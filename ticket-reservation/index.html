⚠️ THE HAZARD vs. HOW FERRICSTORE PREVENTS IT

Why Cart Reservation Timers Fail & How FerricStore Fixes It

Compare standard Redis/Cron failure modes with FerricStore's durable quorum architecture.

👥

Concert Double-Booking

💥 The Hazard (Redis / Cron):

Two users click 'Pay' at the exact second Redis key expires. Both credit cards are charged $280 for Seat A12, resulting in a venue conflict at the gates.

🛡️ How FerricStore Prevents It:

Fenced ownership: after expiry, Buyer #2 advances the durable state with a newer fence. A late Buyer #1 worker cannot overwrite the state; Stripe still requires an idempotency key and current-ownership check.

🚨 $50 Chargeback + PR Disaster ✓ State-Guarded Ownership
🔒

Ghost Inventory Lock Leak

💥 The Hazard (Redis / Cron):

A checkout worker crashes mid-payment without releasing the lock. 50 VIP seats remain marked 'held' forever, going unsold while 5,000 fans wait in line.

🛡️ How FerricStore Prevents It:

Decoupled Raft Timer Heartbeats: The timer runs directly in the replicated consensus cluster, independent of application workers. Even if all checkout workers crash, the timer still fires on schedule and auto-releases the hold.

💸 $14,000 Lost Ticket Sales ✓ Zero Ghost Holds
🔌

Database Connection Storm

💥 The Hazard (Redis / Cron):

Running 50,000 cron polling queries per second to check expired cart timestamps exhausts Postgres connection pools, taking down the entire storefront.

🛡️ How FerricStore Prevents It:

Scheduled eligibility: hold deadlines are durable and can be indexed for due claims. Validate capacity and polling behavior for the intended topology.

📉 Complete Checkout Outage ✓ No Blocked Handler
⚡

Slow Waiting Room Dispatch

💥 The Hazard (Redis / Cron):

Unmanaged carts take 45 seconds of polling latency to reassign abandoned seats, causing waiting room buyers to leave the site in frustration.

🛡️ How FerricStore Prevents It:

Lease-based handoff: after the cart hold expires, the next eligible buyer can claim the durable state with a newer fencing token.

📉 28% Cart Abandonment ✓ Fenced Promotion
